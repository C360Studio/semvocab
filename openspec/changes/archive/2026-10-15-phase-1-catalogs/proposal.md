# phase-1-catalogs

## Why

Issue #1 Phase 1 delivers the first Go content: the `bfo` and `cco` catalogs and the embedded `bfo-cco-core` and
`diekm` packs, registered through SemEngine's pack API. SemSPARQL#1 task 0.1 waits on the SemVocab `v0.1.0` tag that
follows this change. The target state is the archived Phase 0 design
(`openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-15-phase-0-provenance/design.md`, "Phase 1: catalogs and built-in packs") and
`docs/admission-ledger.yaml`, with owner rulings Q1–Q5, Q4b, and Q10 on #1.

## What Changes

- `go.mod` requires `github.com/c360studio/semengine v0.1.0-alpha.2` (`11da4721c2233a8e2b5b1d6236a97a44e06abbb6`),
  a superset of the ledger's alpha.1 pin; the ledger's `pins.semengine` moves to it.
- `catalog/bfo`: the admitted BFO 2020 classes and relations with `SubClassOf`, `Parents`, and `IsA` from the release.
- `catalog/cco`: the admitted CCO v2.2 classes and properties (Go names per the ledger table) with the same hierarchy
  API, crossing into `bfo`.
- `packs/bfo-cco-core.yaml` (prefixes only, Q6) and `packs/diekm/diekm.yaml` with an initial `diekm.shacl.ttl`,
  embedded, with Go registration entry points.
- Vendored ontology oracles (BFO core, CCO v2.2 merged TTL) with their license notices, a test-only Turtle reader,
  and a SemStreams-pin parity fixture generated by a recorded command.
- Capability spec `catalogs-and-packs`; `catalog-provenance` scenario updated for the new SemEngine pin.

## Non-goals

- No SHACL compilation (Phase 2), projection expansion or external SHACL validation (Phase 3), or rule work (Phase 4).
- No change to SemEngine, SemStreams, or SemSPARQL.
- No change to SemEngine's re-registration semantics (D8); tests record them.

## Impact

New Go packages and a SemEngine `require`; a code pull request needing a cross-agent review. After merge, SemVocab
tags `v0.1.0` for SemSPARQL#1 task 0.1.
